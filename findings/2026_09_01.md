## Other values showing up

Patterns that repeat but are not on the list of four.

* **Leave slack in the day** 14:01 "never book a Friday" Two separate scheduling rules, the two hour minimum block and the free Friday, both exist so a job that goes wrong does not wreck a customer's booking. This reads as a distinct operating value about protecting the promise rather than the margin.
* **Price the risk you cannot see** 09:46 "if there's a fire on site anywhere, they'll shut everything down and you'll just sit in the crib room" He builds the cost of unproductive days into remote quotes instead of hoping.
* **Check the record before you accuse** 15:20 "it's another win for the fucking transcriber" He treats his own recordings as the arbiter of who got an order wrong.
* **Ask people who already know** He spends large parts of the day both asking other operators for help on awards and job costing, and giving it back on marketing, quoting and PCB faults. It is a two way habit, not just consuming advice.
